What the frequency evidence buys

One logistic regression, trained once on the three hospitals' rows pooled. The only thing that changes between bars is the answer it is given to “how common is this variant”. Locked test set, 2373 variants.

Both panels, one model, one threshold, one test set. AUC moves by 0.007 and would be invisible on any plot; false alarms fall sevenfold. Frequency does not reorder variants, it vetoes a few — the ones a hospital would otherwise call disease-causing in a patient whose ancestry it rarely sees. A ranking metric is structurally unable to show this, which is why the README section 5 grid reports both.

Federated counts land exactly on the oracle, so exchanging counts loses nothing against being handed gnomAD's real per-population frequencies. A hospital asking only its own patients does worse than today's public reference, because its cohort is a third the size of gnomAD's European set: the gain comes from combining, not from being local. Sensitivity on the 1152 pathogenic test rows stays at 0.92 in every bar, so none of this is bought by missing disease-causing variants.

“Federated counts” here is the step 6 query, each hospital returning carrier counts at scoring time. It is not FedAvg and no NVFlare is involved; federated training is step 4 and is not built yet. Every model in this figure was trained locally.
